# Research Proposal — Version 0.3

## Project Title
The Economics of Commercial Fusion: A Data-Driven Market and Strategic Analysis of Proxima Fusion

## Main Research Question
Under which technological, economic, and market conditions can stellarator-based fusion become commercially competitive in Europe, and what strategic positioning should Proxima Fusion pursue?

## Research Objectives

1. Analyze the structure, financing, and competitive dynamics of the commercial fusion energy industry.
2. Benchmark Proxima Fusion against selected competitors and alternative fusion technologies.
3. Develop a techno-economic model to evaluate the conditions required for competitive fusion electricity generation.
4. Quantify the impact of technological, financial, and market uncertainties.
5. Evaluate alternative commercialization strategies for Proxima Fusion.

## Research Hypotheses

## Scope

- Primary case: Proxima Fusion
- Geographic focus: Germany and Europe
- Competitive comparison: Global fusion industry
- Time horizon: 2035–2050 scenarios
- Methods: Market data analysis, competitive benchmarking, LCOE, discounted cash flow, Monte Carlo simulation, and multi-criteria decision analysis

## Methodological Principles

All quantitative findings must be traceable to documented sources or explicit modeling assumptions.

Company announcements, independently validated technological achievements, and hypothetical projections must be distinguished.

The analysis is an independent academic portfolio project and is not commissioned or endorsed by Proxima Fusion.

## Expected Outputs

- Reproducible Python analyses and datasets
- Techno-economic modeling package
- Interactive dashboard
- Research paper
- Executive strategy presentation

## Research Status — 5 November 2026

Stages 1–2 provide the descriptive financing, partnership and historical market baseline. Stage 3 provides structured engineering and five-company evidence comparison. Stage 4 implements conditional annual LCOE, performance and component-learning sensitivities, legacy project DCF, engineering-constrained generation and construction/replacement lifecycle DCF.

H1 and H3 have conditional mechanism tests, while H2 has descriptive evidence only. All three remain open. The models do not establish actual Stellaris costs, comparative superiority, achieved commercial availability or causal partnership benefits.
