# Stage 5.5 — Integrated Risk Assessment and Research Synthesis

## 1. Research objective

Evaluate whether the techno-economic findings from Stages 3–4 remain informative under exploratory economic uncertainty, alternative marginal distributions, deliberately specified dependence stress cases, and hypothetical magnet-system manufacturing learning.

## 2. Evidence boundaries

The reference electricity-generation and lifecycle models incorporate engineering cases, construction schedules, operating expenditure, and major replacement expenditure. Simulation distributions, dependence structures and magnet learning rates are analyst assumptions, not calibrated likelihoods for Stellaris. The Stage 5.3 extended analysis passed all 336 checks at the 32,768-to-131,072 refinement but retained the official **NONCONVERGED** status under the requirement for two consecutive wholly passing refinements.

## 3. Results to integrate

- **Stage 5.3:** NPV rank sensitivity, within-context quantiles, numerical stability and the unresolved strict convergence requirement.
- **Stage 5.4.1:** Uniform-versus-triangular marginal comparisons, separately for each of 12 contexts and across seeds. Note changes in both distribution shape and some input means.
- **Stage 5.4.2:** Independent versus aligned-burden and offsetting-burden common-rank stress cases. These are *not* fitted correlations or worst/best-case probability bounds.
- **Stage 5.4.3:** Magnet learning across 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 equivalent units, with replacement expenditure held at its unlearned initial-CAPEX basis; learning does not include factory investments or certify NOAK costs.

## 4. Hypothesis interpretation

**H1 (stellarator operating advantage versus construction complexity):** Examine the engineering-context and capital-cost sensitivity relationships. The model supports conditional trade-offs, but does not empirically establish that operational advantages overcome higher capital intensity.

**H2 (partnerships and commercial financing):** Stage 5 illustrates the significance of electricity price, financing and construction risk, but does not test the financial value of actual strategic partnerships. This remains a Stage 6 question.

**H3 (manufacturing scale and standardization):** Learning can reduce the modeled initial magnet-system CAPEX and improve lifecycle outcomes under the assumed functional form. This is a conditional mechanism, not evidence of achieved factory cost reductions or commercial viability.

## 5. Risk priorities for Stage 6

Prioritize empirical CAPEX estimation, offtake/revenue contracting, discount-rate and construction-financing conditions, engineering availability evidence, and the industrial cost of scalable magnet manufacturing. Compare business models using explicit allocations of ownership, cash flows, and development risks; project NPV is not the same as equity value to Proxima.

## 6. Reproducibility and research limitations

The four-seed summary is a replication/robustness diagnostic, not convergence certification. The 12 structural contexts remain unweighted and must not be aggregated into a probability of fusion commercial success. Preserve the original Stage 5.3 convergence audit and the new exploratory extension audit. Ensure revised Stage 1–4 foundation files, accepted uncertainty spec, scripts, tests and hashes are tracked before publication.

## 7. Stage 6 handover

Develop strategic decision alternatives that explicitly identify counterparty, construction owner, development funding responsibility, plant ownership, revenue exposure, license/manufacturing economics and investment downside allocation. Keep all strategic recommendations contingent on verified evidence and transparent scenario assumptions.
