# Stage 3.4 — Evidence-Based Technology Maturity

## Research Objective

Assess the strength and maturity of publicly documented fusion technology achievements, with particular emphasis on Proxima Fusion's commercialization pathway.

**Related hypotheses:** H1, H2 and H3.

## 1. Methodology

Technical milestones are classified by engineering domain, evidence type, achievement status and verification level.

The study distinguishes experimentally achieved performance, modeled results, engineering design specifications and future targets.

A single aggregate technology-readiness score is not assigned because the investigated reactor technologies and projects differ in purpose, technological scope and available evidence.

## 2. Preliminary Findings

Proxima Fusion's published development pathway progresses from conceptual engineering and HTS magnet validation toward the Alpha integrated demonstrator and the proposed Stellaris commercial power plant.

Technical maturity must be evaluated separately for magnetic systems, plasma performance, integrated reactor engineering, fuel and thermal systems, and electrical generation.

Competitors may demonstrate greater progress in individual engineering domains without necessarily possessing a more mature integrated commercial power plant.

## 3. Implications for Research Hypotheses

**H1 — Technology Economics:** Promising simulated performance cannot yet establish realized operational or economic advantages. Future techno-economic modeling should retain explicit uncertainty regarding component performance and availability.

**H2 — Commercialization Strategy:** Technical dependencies may influence when and where partnerships or additional financing are strategically valuable.

**H3 — Manufacturing Scalability:** Manufacturing readiness should be evaluated using demonstrated fabrication, testing and production repeatability—not only proposed standardization.

## 4. Limitations

- Selected milestone records do not provide comprehensive company coverage.
- Company announcements are not necessarily independently verified.
- Technical achievements may not be transferable between reactor designs.
- Project financing and design approval do not demonstrate reactor performance.
- The evidence available as of October 28, 2026 does not establish commercial power-plant performance for the studied concepts.

## 5. Research Outputs

- Milestone classification worksheet
- Engineering evidence coverage matrix
- Achieved-versus-planned milestone summary
- Documented technology development dependencies

## 6. Next Research Step

Preliminary evidence assessment. The maturity comparison does not establish the commercial superiority of any reactor design.
